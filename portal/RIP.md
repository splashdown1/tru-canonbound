# How a page gets into the portal

You do not run a crawler. You do not beat a login. If the text is not already on your screen, it does not go in.

## Phone path

1. Open the listing or article the normal way.
2. Copy the visible text. Headline, price, body. Leave the ads.
3. Open https://splashdown1.github.io/tru-canonbound/portal/portal.html
4. Paste into "Paste a page you already opened". Name the pack for the day you want it readable, not the day you copied it. Example: `2026-10-04 chair`.
5. Tap Keep this text.
6. Ask with a word that is actually in the paste, such as the price or "chair". The portal quotes the paste. If the word is not in it, the gap stays open.

The paste stays in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded.

## Delay

Name the pack for the release day. Three days out, or fourteen. Do not ask it on the day you copied it if the point is the wait. The folder, or the pack name, is the time-release. The portal does not know the news. It only knows what you kept.

## Card shape

```text
2026-10-04 · craigslist
Wooden chair

$40. Pickup in Mobile. Solid seat, one loose rung.
```

## What not to do

Do not point a script at a site you are not looking at. Do not paste a page whose body you could not read. A bookmarklet is optional later, and only for a tab you already opened. The paste box is the path that works on the phone today.
